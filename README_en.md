## What I am building

My current work connects four themes:

- **AI-native workspaces** — conversational data exploration, causal analysis, auditable sessions, and permission boundaries.
- **Personal knowledge systems** — a maintainable content platform built with Astro, Payload CMS, and TypeScript.
- **Mobile products** — `female-solo-life-copilot` is in V0 real-device integration, focused on arrival check-ins and everyday safety routines.
- **Enterprise data engineering** — business metrics, SQL diagnostics, scheduled workflows, and multi-source knowledge ingestion.

The operating principle is simple: **deterministic systems handle reliability; AI handles understanding, generation, and assistance.**

## How I work

- Define boundaries, state transitions, and failure semantics before adding model capabilities.
- Keep critical workflows testable, replayable, and observable instead of relying on “it seems to work”.
- Prefer TypeScript and Python, with Vue, React Native, SQL, and Docker where they fit the problem.
- Document the work around the code: architecture decisions, product reviews, runbooks, and reusable tools.
